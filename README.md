## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

main problems:
- Multiple intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.
- Low income for farmers.
- High prices for consumers.
- Difficulty finding direct buyers.
- High transportation expenses.
- Unpredictable weather conditions.
- Crop damage and unexpected supply shortages.
- Wastage of unsold agricultural products.


Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AGRI-LOOP – A Smart Agricultural Supply Chain Platform
- Connect farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Reduce unnecessary intermediaries.
- Improve farmers' earnings.
- Provide affordable products to consumers.

Main Features

1. Direct Connection
- Farmer registration.
- Crop listing.
- Direct buyer connection.
- Bulk orders.
2. Demand & Harvest Prediction
- Future demand estimation.
- Expected harvest quantity.
- Pre-harvest reservations.
- Supply availability updates.
3. Weather Risk Monitoring – Prevention
- Weather updates.
- Heavy rainfall alerts.
- Crop condition monitoring.
- Early shortage warnings.
4. Backup Supply – Recovery
- Find alternative farmers.
- Search nearby markets.
- Identify replacement suppliers.
- Partial delivery or cancellation.
- Refund management.
5. Farmer Grouping & Shared Transport
- Combine small farmers' produce.
- Fulfil bulk orders.
- Shared vehicle arrangements.
- Reduce transportation costs.
6. Fair Price System
- Market price comparison.
- Transparent price breakdown.
- Better selling decisions.
7. Surplus Management
- Identify unsold products.
- Find alternative buyers.
- Discount surplus produce.
- Reduce food wastage.
